I am a PhD student in the HeKA team at INRIA, supervised by [François-Xavier Vialard](http://angkor.univ-mlv.fr/~vialard/) and [Jean Feydy](https://www.jeanfeydy.com).

My work explores geometric approaches to 3D medical image processing and visualization, with a primary focus on optimal transport methods for bone fracture registration.
More broadly, I am interested in computational geometry and its practical applications.
